About SleepyMie

Heya, I'm SleepyMie! 👋

I really, really love learning and exploring new things! I'm good at noticing when something's off, or when a system starts doing things it shouldn't, and I like finding the best fix I can, especially one that makes work faster. I also love digging into my tools until I understand them well enough to get the most out of them.

But I care about more than whether something works. I care about how it feels to the player. Is this feature something people actually enjoy, or want? I especially love supporting low-end devices, because I've been that player myself, and I want everyone to be able to join in and have fun.

Games are a big part of why I'm here. Palworld, Terraria and Minecraft inspired me to make fun games of my own, and I'm a huge fan of indie games like Deltarune, Undertale and Doki Doki Literature Club. I love how they connect with players and pull you into their worlds.

In my own games, I keep a close eye on how people actually play, and I try to make sure their needs are met. When players keep asking for something, I bring it to the team with evidence and let them weigh it.

Curious about the game and the team? Visit the ChocoNeko team site.

How I work

  • I test it myself before calling it fixed. I go back into the game, usually in both Studio and the published version, instead of taking "it works" on faith.
  • I chase the real cause. When a backpack bug only showed up for some players, I had a friend rejoin and respawn until it was clear it happened on join.
  • I think about the player. Flicker, lag and how a game runs on lower-end devices matter to me as much as whether a feature technically works.
