Bug report 1 · Retrospective write-up of a real bug, now fixed

CN-BUG-001: HUD Phone and Backpack panels open and immediately close on click (about 4 in 10 clicks, depends on the join)

ID
CN-BUG-001
Date found
Sep 3, 2026
Reported by
SleepyMie (first spotted by a friend playtesting the live game, which is published but not yet publicly launched)
Severity
High. Two core HUD buttons were unreliable in the live game. Workaround: click again.
Priority
P1. Affects every player who joins into the bad state.
Component
HUD: Phone button, Backpack button
Status
Closed: fixed and verified (Sep 3, 2026)

Environment

  • Build: [version]
  • Environment: live (published) game, also checked in Roblox Studio
  • Place: [place where observed]
  • Platform: PC, mouse input. Device: [device model]. OS: [OS version]
  • Second tester: a friend on their own machine, live game

Preconditions

  • Player has joined the game and the HUD has finished loading.
  • Phone and Backpack panels are closed.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Join the live game.
  2. When the HUD appears, click the Phone button once.
  3. Watch the Phone panel for about 2 seconds, then close it if it opened.
  4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 ten times and count how many clicks leave the panel open.
  5. Repeat steps 2 to 4 with the Backpack button.
  6. Leave and rejoin, then repeat steps 2 to 5.
  7. Respawn the character, then repeat steps 2 to 5.

Expected result

Each click opens the panel and it stays open until the player closes it.

Actual result

On roughly 4 in 10 clicks the panel opens and closes again straight away, so it looks like the button did nothing. How often it happens depends on the join: in some sessions the buttons worked fine, and one tester could open them without problems while it failed for another. During later retests a new pattern showed up where only one of the two buttons worked.

Reproduction rate

About 4 in 10 clicks in affected sessions (my rough count). Not every session is affected; it depends on the join.

Evidence / attachments

  • [CLIP: clicking Phone and Backpack repeatedly]
  • [Log: click probe output, before and after the fix]

Notes

  • Narrowing down: I had my friend rejoin and respawn to test a few theories, and concluded the problem started on join rather than from anything later in the session.
  • I hadn't changed these two buttons in a while, so I suspected another change had broken them.
  • Cause, as found by the coding agent over three fixes: (1) a timing issue in how the buttons were wired up on join; (2) one click was being handled twice, so the panel toggled open and then closed; (3) the panel opened on mouse-down, and the dark full-screen background behind it closed it again on mouse-up. Larger panels hid the problem because they cover the top of the screen.
  • I asked for a click probe (logging every step of a click) to confirm the final fix. The probe was left in the code but switched off, so it can be turned back on if the bug returns.

Status / verification

Closed: fixed and verified (Sep 3, 2026)

FixMy retest
Fix 1 (join timing)Retested: still failing.
Fix 2 (click handled twice)Retested: still intermittent.
Fix 3 (mouse-down / mouse-up)Retested: a new pattern where sometimes only one of the two buttons worked. Requested a click probe.
Final checkProbe logs showed the full click path completing on every click, and both panels opened and stayed open in my retest. Closed.

Bug report 2 · Retrospective write-up of a real bug, now fixed

CN-BUG-002: Incrediblox: free cam takes about 5 seconds to open after pressing Cam (5,032 ms in timing log)

ID
CN-BUG-002
Date found
Sep 11, 2026
Reported by
SleepyMie
Severity
Medium. The feature works in the end, but there is a long, unexplained wait with no feedback.
Priority
P2. Noticeable delay on a player-facing feature, no data loss or crash.
Component
Incrediblox: free cam (Cam button); also related to the phone
Status
Closed: fixed and verified (Sep 11, 2026)

Environment

  • Build: [version]
  • Environment: Roblox Studio playtest (timing log captured here). Live: [confirm whether also checked live]
  • Place: Incrediblox
  • Platform: PC. Device: [device model]. OS: [OS version]
  • Compared against: Hub place, where the same shared code did not have this delay

Preconditions

  • Player is in the Incrediblox place and fully loaded.
  • Free cam is closed.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Start a playtest in the Incrediblox place.
  2. Wait for the HUD to finish loading.
  3. Press the Cam button.
  4. Time how long it takes until the free cam controls respond (use the timing log between the click and "free cam ready").
  5. Close free cam and repeat a few times.
  6. Open the phone and note whether it has a similar delay.

Expected result

Free cam opens straight away after pressing Cam, apart from the short intended overlay animation.

Actual result

Free cam takes about 5 seconds to become usable. The timing log shows 5,032 ms between the click and "free cam ready". The phone showed a similar lag in this place.

Reproduction rate

[x of y attempts; exact count not recorded at the time]

Evidence / attachments

  • [CLIP: free cam opening, before vs after]
  • [Log: timing log showing 5,032 ms between click and free cam ready]

Notes

  • The first fix removed an intentional overlay delay of about 3 seconds. I retested and the long delay was still there, so I asked for timing logs between the click and "free cam ready", ran them and pasted the output.
  • Because the phone had a similar lag, I asked the agent to compare against the Hub place.
  • Cause, as found by the coding agent: code shared with the phone waited up to 5 seconds for a player-controls module that wasn't where it expected in this place (in Incrediblox it sat in a different location than in Hub). The 5,032 ms in the log matched that 5-second wait.
  • Fix: the code no longer waits and checks both locations. On the agent's advice I also turned on a Studio setting for the default player module.

Status / verification

Closed: fixed and verified (Sep 11, 2026)

FixMy retest
Fix 1 (removed ~3 s overlay delay)Retested: still slow. Requested timing logs.
Fix 2 (no waiting, checks both locations, plus Studio setting)Retested: Cam opened straight away. Closed.

CN-TP-001: Test plan: HUD and chat day/night recolour (regression)

Plan ID
CN-TP-001
Build under test
[version]
Tester
SleepyMie
Dates
[start date] to [end date]

Summary of the change

The always-on HUD now changes colour gradually with the in-game day/night cycle so bright themes aren't glaring in daytime. Other panels, such as the chat, phone and backpack, pick up the day/night colours when they're opened instead of updating all the time. Players can turn this off with a toggle in the Theme tab (on by default). This plan checks that the change works, doesn't flicker, stays cheap on frame time, and hasn't broken anything nearby.

In scope

  • Always-on HUD colours during day, night and the transitions between them.
  • Chat panel colours and text readability (including the contrast stroke) at day and night.
  • Panels that recolour when opened (phone, backpack, others).
  • Theme tab toggle, on and off, and its default.
  • All five themes: Chocolate, Dark Choco, Vanilla, Strawberry, Blueberry.
  • Frame time of the recolour in Studio.
  • Rejoin and respawn behaviour.
  • Mobile HUD layout after the change.

Out of scope

  • The day/night lighting itself (sky, sun, world lighting).
  • New theme designs or colour choices (design review, not regression).
  • Chat features unrelated to colour (sending, whispers, filtering).
  • Detailed performance profiling on specific low-end devices.

Environments

Roblox Studio
PC playtest and Studio phone emulator. Used for frame-time checks.
Live (published) game
PC [device model / OS] and mobile phone [device model / OS].
Places
Hub, TD Survival, Incrediblox, Cooking (Cooking uses the Hub HUD).
Tools
In-game /time admin command to jump between day and night; Studio MicroProfiler or a timing log for frame time; screen recording for flicker checks.

Entry criteria

  • The change is published to the test place and the build number is recorded.
  • The developer's own smoke test passes (HUD loads, no errors in the Output log).
  • Tester has admin access for the /time command.

Exit criteria

  • All P1 cases pass in both Studio and live.
  • No open High or Critical bugs against this change.
  • Recolour frame time in Studio is close to the ~2–3 ms reference (exact threshold: [ms]).
  • No visible flicker in screen recordings on PC or mobile.
  • Any P2/P3 failures are logged with a decision (fix now or later).
